Sliq — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sliq ("Sliq", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") may access or store when you use the Sliq Discord application.

1. Information We May Access

Because Sliq operates through Discord, it may receive information made available to Discord applications based on the permissions and intents granted to it. This can include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, usernames, display names, avatars, roles, permissions, command interactions, and information needed to perform requested features.

Sliq does not intentionally request or store Discord account passwords, authentication tokens, payment card information, or private messages unless a feature explicitly requires information that Discord makes available through an authorized interaction.

2. Information We Store

Sliq may store limited information needed to provide its features, such as server configuration, enabled features, channel or role settings, moderation configuration, and anonymous or identifier-based records required for bot functionality.

We aim to collect and retain only information reasonably necessary for the operation of Sliq.

3. Message Content

If a Sliq feature requires access to message content, Sliq may process message content temporarily to perform that feature. Message content is not intentionally retained as a permanent database record unless a specific feature requires storage and that storage is clearly communicated to users.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate commands, maintain server configuration, provide moderation and utility features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve reliability and functionality.

We do not sell user information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent Sliq user data. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers necessary to operate Sliq, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Configuration and operational data may be retained for as long as it is needed to provide Sliq's features. When data is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized.

Server owners may request deletion of Sliq's stored configuration for their server by contacting the Sliq support team through the official Sliq support server.

7. Discord

Sliq is a third-party Discord application. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Discord controls information collected directly by Discord.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information used by Sliq. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Sliq does not knowingly request personal information from children outside what is made available through Discord and necessary for the application's operation. Users must comply with Discord's minimum-age requirements and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Sliq's features or data practices change. The current version will always be published on this page with the latest update date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Sliq support team through the official Sliq support server.

Sliq is an independent third-party Discord application and is not operated by Discord Inc.
